# CHAPTER IV
## RESULTS AND DISCUSSIONS

This chapter presents the results and discussion of the study. It focuses on how the eFlora system for multi-vendor flower shops and plant nurseries was implemented and how it was assessed by the respondents and IT experts. The results are presented through figures and tables, followed by an interpretation of the data gathered throughout the evaluation.

#### 1.2. Established a seller dashboard and multi-channel revenue monitoring module.

**[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER]**

**[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER]**
> **Image to insert:** `Image of Products Catalog Page (products.html) - Web`

**Figure 22   Product Catalog and Multi-Variant Inventory Management Page**

Figure 22 presents the Product and Inventory Management module in eFlora. The interface displays real-time stock levels, SKU identifiers, base pricing, and low-stock warning indicators, allowing merchants to execute manual stock reductions, record restocking batches, and ensure inventory synchronization across both online and walk-in sales channels.

The right side features a dynamic order summary panel that calculates subtotals, applies discounts or custom fees, computes exact change, and processes payments via Cash or in-store GCash QR transfers.

**[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER]**

**Figure 33   Rider Navigation and Delivery Status Progression**

Figure 33 presents the active Delivery Progress screen in the rider app. The interface features integrated GPS navigation mapping the optimal route from the flower shop to the recipient's doorstep. #### 3.3. Delivery completion and proof of delivery.

**[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER]**
> **Image to insert:** `Image of Rider Delivery Completion and Proof of Delivery Screen - App`

**Figure 34   Rider Order Completion and Proof of Delivery**

Figure 34 illustrates the Order Completion screen in the rider mobile app. To ensure accountability and customer satisfaction, the system requires the courier to capture a Proof of Delivery (POD) photo showing the delivered flower bouquet or potted plant received by the recipient or placed at the secure drop-off point. For COD transactions, the rider inputs the collected cash amount before submitting the final completion form. Once submitted, the system automatically marks the order as Completed across the customer app and seller dashboard, releasing delivery fee earnings to the rider's wallet.
