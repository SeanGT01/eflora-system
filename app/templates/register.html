Customer Terms & Conditions

1. Customer Agreement

By creating an E-FLORA customer account, you agree to comply with and be bound by these terms. They follow our public customer policies for shipping and returns. Please read them carefully.

2. Account Registration

You must provide accurate information when registering. You are responsible for keeping your login details confidential and for activity on your account.

3. Shipping & Delivery

When you order, a nearby partner shop prepares your arrangement and delivers it (or hands it to a rider) within their service area. Delivery fees and windows depend on the shop and your address.

  • Coverage is based on each store’s delivery zone — usually cities and barangays across Laguna.
  • You’ll see whether a shop can deliver to your saved address before checkout.
  • Many shops offer same-day or next-day slots when they’re open and capacity allows.

4. Delivery Windows & Timing

Pick an available time slot at checkout when the shop offers them. Same-day availability depends on cut-off times, shop hours, and rider capacity. If a slot isn’t open, choose the next available window.

Share a clear address, landmark, and working phone number. If nobody can receive the order, the shop or rider may call to arrange a safe handoff or reschedule per the shop’s policy.

5. Fees & Tracking

Delivery fees are set by each partner shop and shown before you pay. After checkout, track progress from My Orders — from preparing to out for delivery. For a late or missing delivery, use order chat or contact support with your order number.

6. Returns & Refunds

Because arrangements are made to order and spoil quickly, we generally don’t accept returns of flowers that were delivered as described and in good condition. We do help when there’s a clear delivery or quality problem.

7. Eligible Issues

Contact us promptly — ideally within 24 hours of delivery — if:

  • The order never arrived or was delivered to the wrong address due to a fulfillment error.
  • Blooms arrived wilted, crushed, or clearly damaged in transit.
  • You received the wrong product or a missing item from your order.
  • The arrangement is substantially different from what was listed (not minor natural variation).

Photos of the delivery and your order number help us resolve things faster with the shop.

8. What Usually Isn’t Covered

  • Change of mind after the arrangement has been prepared or delivered.
  • Normal variation in bloom size, shade, or filler flowers (seasonal and natural).
  • Issues caused by incorrect recipient details you provided.
  • Flowers left unattended outdoors or without water after a successful handoff.

9. How to Request Help

Open the order in My Orders and message the shop, or contact E-FLORA support. Share your order number, what went wrong, and clear photos when relevant. We’ll coordinate with the partner shop on a replacement, partial refund, or full refund when appropriate.

10. Cancellations

You can cancel from My Orders while the shop hasn’t started preparing. Once an order is accepted and in preparation, cancellation may no longer be available — contact the shop or support as soon as possible if plans change.

11. Modifications to Terms

We may update these terms to match our public Shipping and Returns pages. Continued use of your account after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.

12. Contact Information

For questions, contact support at efloralaguna@gmail.com. Full policy pages: Shipping and Returns.
