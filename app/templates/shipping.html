How delivery works

From nearby partner shop to your door.

When you order, a nearby partner shop prepares your arrangement and delivers it (or hands it to a rider) within their service area. Delivery fees and windows depend on the shop and your address.

  • Coverage is based on each store’s delivery zone — usually cities and barangays across Laguna.
  • You’ll see whether a shop can deliver to your saved address before checkout.
  • Many shops offer same-day or next-day slots when they’re open and capacity allows.

Delivery windows & timing

Pick a slot that works for the recipient.

Scheduled delivery

Pick an available time slot at checkout when the shop offers them. We’ll show the shop’s preparation and delivery guidance on the store page.

Same-day orders

Same-day availability depends on cut-off times, shop hours, and rider capacity. If a slot isn’t open, choose the next available window.

Recipient not home

Share a clear address, landmark, and working phone number. If nobody can receive the order, the shop or rider may call to arrange a safe handoff or reschedule per the shop’s policy.

Fees & tracking

No surprises at the door.

Delivery fees are set by each partner shop and shown before you pay. After checkout, track progress from My Orders — from preparing to out for delivery.

Need help with a late or missing delivery? Open the order chat, message the shop, or contact E-FLORA support with your order number.

Tips for a smooth delivery

Small details that keep blooms happy.

  • Keep your default address and phone number up to date in your profile.
  • Add gate codes, floor, or landmark notes when placing the order.
  • Choose a delivery time when someone can receive perishable flowers.
